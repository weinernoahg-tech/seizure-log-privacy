Privacy Policy

EpiShield · Last updated 20 September 2026

EpiShield does not collect your data. Everything you record stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no servers, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

This policy explains that in full. It applies to the EpiShield app for iPhone.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. We have no way to see what you record in the app, because it never leaves your device.

We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address or phone number.

Information you enter

The app lets you record things like seizure dates, times, durations, types and notes; the medications you take, including doses and schedules; and daily entries about how you are feeling.

All of this is stored locally in the app's own storage on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot read it, recover it, or restore it for you.

If your iPhone is set to back up to iCloud, your device backup may include the app's data. That backup is controlled by Apple under your own iCloud account and settings, not by us. You can change what is backed up in the Settings app on your iPhone.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

There are none. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and there is nothing in the app that identifies you or your device to us.

Health data

The app does not connect to Apple Health, and does not read from or write to HealthKit. It does not use your iPhone's sensors, and it does not attempt to detect, measure, or predict seizures. It is a diary you fill in yourself.

Sharing your own records

If you choose to export or share your records — for example to show them to a doctor — that sharing happens on your device using the standard iOS share options, and you decide where it goes. We are not involved in it and do not receive a copy.

Children

The app is a personal record-keeping tool and can be used by or on behalf of a child, often by a parent or carer keeping a record for their family. Because the app collects nothing at all, no information about a child is collected, transmitted, or stored by us.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone removes its stored data from your device. Because we never hold a copy, there is nothing for you to request the deletion of, and no account to close.

Deletion is permanent. If you want to keep your records, export them before deleting the app.

Not medical advice

EpiShield is a record-keeping tool. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, does not provide medical advice, and does not calculate or recommend medication doses. Always talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your care, and never change how you take a medication based on anything in this app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a change in what happens to your information.
